Speak into any text field. Nothing leaves the phone.

Voicemator is dictation for Android. Tap, talk, tap again, and the words are typed where your cursor is. The speech model runs on the phone itself, so it works in airplane mode and nobody else hears you.

01 · How you use it

Three ways to start talking, and you can use all of them.

Whichever you pick, a short buzz tells you it's listening, a double buzz tells you it stopped, and a small pill around the camera shows your voice while you speak.

  1. A

    The Quick Settings tile

    Pull down the shade, tap Dictate. When you stop, the text lands on the clipboard and in a notification. There is also a home-screen widget and a shortcut on the app icon.

  2. B

    A mic button at the keyboard

    Turn on the accessibility service and a small button shows up whenever a keyboard is open, in any app. Dictate, and the text is typed straight into the field. Drag the button where your thumb wants it.

  3. C

    Your keyboard's own mic key

    Voicemator registers as the system's voice input. With FUTO Keyboard, or any keyboard that hands its mic key to the system, that key now opens Voicemator, and you're back at the keyboard when you're done.

02 · Privacy

Your voice goes from the microphone to the model and nowhere else.

Microphone16 kHz mono
Memorynever written to disk
Parakeeton the phone's CPU
Textin your field

cloud account analytics subscription

The app goes online exactly once: to download the speech model, about 670 MB, over Wi-Fi unless you say otherwise. The file is checked against pinned SHA-256 checksums before it is used. After that you can switch the network off.

The history of your dictations stays on the phone, and you decide how much of it there is: everything, only the numbers (word counts and times, no text), or nothing at all.

The code is open, so none of this has to be taken on trust.

03 · Details

The small things that make you use it every day.

Live preview
The words show up while you are still speaking. Long dictations are transcribed in pieces as you go, so the text is ready the moment you stop.
A dictionary of your words
Teach it the names it keeps getting wrong: voice mator → Voicemator.
App profiles
No capital letter and no full stop in messengers, full punctuation in email. Each app can have its own dictionary entries too.
No “um”
Hesitation sounds like um, eee and the Polish yyy are dropped before the text reaches you. You can turn this off.
A final pass
If you want the best punctuation, Voicemator can read the whole recording again as one piece after you stop. It takes a few seconds.
Voice messages and recordings
Share a voice note from WhatsApp or Messenger, or open any audio file, however long. Save the result as text or as SRT subtitles.
A hands-free toggle
Set the accessibility shortcut, for example holding both volume keys, and dictation starts without looking at the screen.
Statistics
Words today and in total, a streak, your speaking pace, and roughly how much time you saved compared with typing.
04 · Languages

25 European languages, no language switch.

05 · Install

Not on Google Play. Here is what you need instead.

Voicemator is installed from an APK published on GitHub, built and signed by GitHub Actions straight from the source.

SystemAndroid 14 or newer
Processor64-bit ARM, which is any recent phone
StorageAbout 1 GB, mostly the model. You can delete it in Settings.
MemoryRoughly 1 GB of RAM while transcribing
PriceFree, no ads, no in-app purchases
  1. Download the APK and open it. Android will ask you to allow installs from your browser.
  2. Open Voicemator, allow the microphone and notifications, and download the model.
  3. Add the Dictate tile to Quick Settings.
  4. For the keyboard button, turn on Voicemator dictation under Settings → Accessibility. If Android says the setting is restricted, open App info, tap ⋮ and allow restricted settings first.

Try talking to your phone for a week.
